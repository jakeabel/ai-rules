# Optional work records

Use a record when it helps review, preserves a non-obvious decision, or supports a handoff. A small, clear task does not need all of these documents. Keep a record near the owning project, in the location that project's tracking notes name. Shared standards stay in `~/.ai`. Project facts stay in the project overlay.

## Substantial change plan

- Outcome and observable acceptance criteria:
- Owning repository, implementation, and affected consumers:
- Existing behavior and source evidence:
- Proposed change and compatibility implications:
- Security, data, and external effects where applicable:
- Verification commands and prerequisites:
- Unresolved decisions and interim behavior:

## Bug investigation

- Reproduction and expected versus actual behavior:
- Evidence and root cause:
- Smallest coherent fix:
- Regression check and result:
- Remaining limitations:

## Architecture decision

- Title, date, status (proposed, accepted, or superseded), and owner:
- Problem and constraints:
- Chosen approach and alternatives considered:
- Tradeoffs, compatibility, and consequences:
- Affected files or services and verification:
- Decision it supersedes, if any:

## Handoff or completion

- Outcome and changed behavior:
- Files and repositories affected:
- Checks actually run, with results:
- Checks not run, and why:
- Remaining decisions or the next action:

For an unresolved human decision, add the exact `## todo human` heading to the record, followed by the question, the evidence, and the temporary behavior. Do not mark an unimplemented change as done.
